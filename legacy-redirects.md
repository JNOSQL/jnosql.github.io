The Jakarta NoSQL specification and API documentation are maintained by the Jakarta EE project.
See [Jakarta NoSQL](https://jakarta.ee/specifications/nosql/) and the [source repository](https://github.com/jakartaee/nosql).

Historical copies of the 1.0.0-M1 and 1.0 specification and Javadoc previously hosted on this site were removed. The versioned URLs (for example `/spec/1.0/` and `/javadoc/1.0/`) are not redirected because they cannot be mapped to exact official destinations.
